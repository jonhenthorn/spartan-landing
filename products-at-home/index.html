Before you order

At-home product questions.

Who takes payment and ships the order?

Herbalife is the merchant of record for the external storefront. It processes payment and taxes, ships products from its warehouse, and handles order support, returns and refunds. Spartan does not personally pack or ship these orders.

Do I have to buy a paid membership?

No paid VIP membership is required to browse the product store. A free account is available for rewards and account management. VIP savings are optional and have separate current terms and pricing.

Is Preferred Member still available?

The current name shown by the provider is Wellness Rewards VIP, and its official materials describe it as the program formerly called Preferred Member. Use the current rewards page for up-to-date pricing, discounts and enrollment terms.

Do I need ongoing coaching to reorder?

No. You can sign in and reorder independently. Spartan remains available for basic product questions, but this page is not a coaching or consultation signup.

Is this the same as ordering a prepared drink?

No. Prepared drinks and local Mega Tea Kits come from Spartan in Bixby. Full-size shipped products are ordered, paid for and fulfilled through the external product storefront.
