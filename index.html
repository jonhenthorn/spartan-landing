Before you visit

Frequently asked questions.

What is Spartan Nutrition?

Spartan Nutrition is a locally owned, veteran-owned nutrition club in Bixby. We’ve served the community since 2016 with made-to-order energy teas, protein shakes, coffee-style drinks and seasonal releases.

Are energy teas caffeinated?

Many are. Caffeine can vary by drink and customization, so ask the team before ordering. Low-caffeine or caffeine-free options may be available.

Do you have options for kids?

Yes. Ask the team about current kid-friendly and caffeine-free choices.

Can I call ahead?

Yes. Call (918) 928-9755 to place a prepared-drink order for pickup. Online prepared-drink ordering is not currently advertised.

Can I make Spartan-style drinks at home?

Ask about currently available mega tea kits and other home-use options. Pickup and ordering methods depend on the product, and the team can point you to the appropriate path.

Do you have dairy-free, vegan or gluten-free options?

Some drinks may be customizable. Ingredients can change, so please discuss allergies and dietary needs with the team before ordering.
